01 · THE NAME

The word on our sign.

أصلي بيروت

In Arabic, asli (أصلي) means original. For us it means Lebanese food the way it's made at home. The cedar beside it is Lebanon's.

02 · THE GRILL

Grilled to order, over charcoal.

عالفحم، وعالطلب.

Shish taouk, kafta, meat cubes, lamb chops, arayes, grilled chicken and the mixed grills: all grilled to order over charcoal. The shawarma is sliced off the spit. All our food is halal certified.

03 · THE FAMILY

A family kitchen.

مطبخ العيلة

Asli Beirut is run by our family. We've been cooking on the First Floor of Dubai Hills Mall since 2022, and we still cook the way we eat at home.

04 · THE TABLE

Made for sharing.

سفرة وحدة، والكل حواليها.

The Mixed Grills 1 kg is kafta, shish taouk and meat cubes on one platter for the whole table. It's AED 189, and it goes in the middle.
